답변 공식 · 연결 표현

어떤 질문이든 도입 한 줄 → 디테일 2~3개 → 마무리 한 줄. 한 문항 60~90초, 8~12문장이 목표예요.

질문 유형뼈대시제
묘사한 줄 요약 → 특징 3가지 → 내 느낌현재
습관·루틴얼마나 자주 → 순서대로 단계 → 왜 좋은지현재
과거 경험언제·어디서·누구와 → 사건 순서 → 결과·느낀 점과거
비교·변화예전엔 → 지금은 → 바뀐 이유과거+현재
의견·계획내 생각 → 이유 2개 → 앞으로 계획현재+미래

연결 표현 10개

  • 시작: Well, let me think. / To be honest, / Actually,
  • 순서: First of all, / After that, / Finally,
  • 이유: The reason is that... / That's why...
  • 추가: Also, / Another thing is that...
  • 마무리: Overall, I really enjoy it. / It was a great memory for me.

막혔을 때 채움말: How can I put it... / What I wanted to say is... / I mean,
단어가 안 떠오르면 쉬운 말로 풀기. 예: 서랍장 → a small cabinet with drawers

주제별 스크립트 6개

가린 상태에서 스크립트를 누르면 그 부분만 잠깐 보여요.

#1 자기소개 (1번 문항, 거의 확정)

뼈대: 직업 → 가족 → 취미 → 최근 일 → 시험 목표

Hi, my name is [이름], and I'm a software developer. I work at a company where I develop and manage an internal settlement system, so I spend most of my day at a computer. I live with my wife and my three-year-old son, Junhyuk. He is very energetic, so our home is never quiet! In my free time, I like watching movies and taking short walks with my family. Last month, I started thinking about rearranging our house, and I'm really excited about it. I'm taking this test because I want to improve my English speaking skill, and I hope to do well today. Thank you.

#2 우리 집 묘사

뼈대: 사는 곳 → 큰 방 → 컴퓨터방 → 좋아하는 이유 → 마무리 (계획으로 미래 시제 한 번)

I live in an apartment with my wife and my son. The biggest room is our bedroom, and it has a large bed in the middle. Actually, I'm planning to put up a partition wall there so that we can use the space better. We also have a computer room, which is my favorite place. It has my desk, a bookshelf, and a few of my son's books. The reason I like it is that it is quiet, so I can focus. Overall, it's not a big house, but it's cozy and comfortable for our family.

#3 직장 하루 루틴 (습관형)

뼈대: 기상 → 출근 → 오전 업무 → 오후 업무 → 퇴근, 이유

On weekdays, I usually wake up at around seven. First of all, I get ready and drop by a cafe for coffee. After that, I arrive at the office and check the system to see if there are any errors. Then I spend the morning fixing problems and answering questions from other teams. After lunch, I focus on development work, like writing new features. Finally, I leave the office at around six and go home to my family. The reason I like this routine is that I can feel productive, and I can still spend time with my son in the evening.

#4 가족과 보낸 주말 (과거 경험)

뼈대: 언제·어디서·누구와 → 날씨 → 사건 순서 → 결과·느낀 점 (전부 과거형)

Let me think. Last weekend, I went to a park with my wife and my son. The weather was really nice, so we decided to take a walk after lunch. When we arrived, my son saw a big playground and ran straight to it. He played on the slide again and again, and he was laughing the whole time. After that, we bought some ice cream and sat on a bench. Later, he fell asleep in the car on our way home. It was a simple day, but I felt really happy. That's why I remember it as a great memory.

#5 해외여행 (계획 + 과거 경험)

뼈대: 좋아하는 이유 → 미래 계획(be going to) → 과거 여행(과거형) → 기대

I love traveling abroad with my family. This December, we're going to travel to Guam for the first time. The reason I chose Guam is that it has beautiful beaches and it's good for a young child. I'm going to rent a car, and I'm planning to spend a lot of time at the beach. Actually, last year we went to Jeju Island, and it was a great trip. We stayed at a hotel near the ocean, and my son built a sand castle for the first time. Even though it rained on the second day, we had a wonderful time together. I'm really looking forward to the next trip.

#6 영화 보기 (습관 + 비교·변화)

뼈대: 빈도 → 방법 → 취향 → 예전 vs 지금(used to) → 이유

I usually watch movies at home on weekends. Since my son was born, it has been difficult to go to the theater, so I use a streaming service instead. After he falls asleep, my wife and I choose a movie together. I prefer action movies, and she likes romantic comedies, so we take turns. A few years ago, I used to go to the cinema every month, but now I watch at home. Actually, I think the home is more comfortable because I can pause the movie anytime. That's why I enjoy watching movies at home these days.

롤플레이 · 돌발

롤플레이는 보통 3문항: ① 상대에게 질문하기 ② 문제 상황에서 대안 제시 ③ 비슷한 경험 말하기.

① 질문하기 (질문 3~5개)

Hello, I'd like to ask a few questions about [주제]. First, could you tell me what time/where it starts? Also, how much does it cost? And is there anything I should bring? One more thing, do I need to make a reservation? Thank you so much.

② 문제 해결 (대안 2개)

I'm so sorry to hear that, but don't worry. There are two things we can do. First, I can [대안 1]. If that doesn't work, then I can [대안 2]. Which one do you prefer? I'm sure we can solve this problem.

예: 호텔 예약이 잘못됨 → I can change to a different room type. / I can come back tomorrow at the same time.

③ 비슷한 경험 (과거형)

Actually, something similar happened to me before. It was last year when I [상황]. At first, I was [감정], but I [해결 방법]. In the end, it turned out okay, and I learned that [교훈].

돌발 주제 (은행, 기술, 약속, 재활용 등)

  1. Well, that's a good question. 으로 시간 벌기
  2. 내 생활과 가장 가까운 경험 고르기 (기술 → 업무 시스템, 은행 → 모바일 뱅킹)
  3. 습관형 또는 과거 경험 공식에 그대로 대입
  4. 막히면 It's difficult to explain, but... 으로 쉬운 단어로 돌려 말하기

시험 직전 체크리스트

당일 (11/20)
